5-Minute Mug Cake

⏱ 5 min · 🍽 serves 1

Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 3 tbsp oil
Steps
  1. Mix all ingredients in a mug.
  2. Microwave for 90 seconds.
  3. Let cool slightly, then eat.

Loaded Quesadilla

⏱ 10 min · 🍽 serves 2

Ingredients
  • 2 tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/2 cup beans
  • salsa
Steps
  1. Layer cheese and beans on a tortilla.
  2. Top with second tortilla.
  3. Cook in a pan 2-3 min per side until golden.

Fruity Yogurt Bowl

⏱ 5 min · 🍽 serves 1

Ingredients
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1/2 cup berries
  • granola
  • honey
Steps
  1. Add yogurt to a bowl.
  2. Top with berries and granola.
  3. Drizzle honey on top.
