5-Minute Mug Cake

5 min · serves 1

tap to see recipe →

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp flour
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 3 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Mix all ingredients in a mug.
  2. Microwave for 90 seconds.
  3. Let cool slightly, then eat.

Homemade Fried Rice

20 min · serves 3

tap to see recipe →

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice, day-old is best
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup mixed veggies
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Steps

  1. Saute veggies for 3-4 min.
  2. Push veggies aside, scramble eggs in the same pan.
  3. Add rice, soy sauce, and sesame oil, stir-fry 5 min.
  4. Mix everything together and serve.
